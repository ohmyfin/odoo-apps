Sanctions & Export Check

Checks every sales order before you confirm it: the customer, the destination and the goods.

The customer and the delivery address are screened against sanctions lists, the delivery country against embargoes and the Russia rules, and every order line against export controls by its HS code. Odoo already holds all three, so nobody types anything.

The party

The customer and the ship-to name, screened against more than 300 sanctions lists, among them OFAC SDN, the EU consolidated list, UK OFSI and the UN lists. A result reads "No match on N lists, with the date", "Potential match" or "Listed".

The destination

The delivery country, against embargoes and country export restrictions. For an EU seller, a buyer outside the EU's partner countries is where the Russia rules of Regulation 833/2014 apply.

The goods

Every order line by the product's HS code and country of origin, against export controls and dual-use lists. Common high priority (CHP) items to a country outside the partner list are flagged for the due diligence Art. 12gb asks for, with the Art. 12g no-re-export clause as the action.

What happens on each result

Result on the orderWhat Odoo does
Listed party, embargoed destination or prohibited goodsConfirm is refused, with the reason on the order.
Potential match, licence may be required, or a CHP item to a diversion-risk countryThe order waits until a user in the group "Ohmyfin: compliance reviewer" records a written reason. The reason and the report id go to the chatter.
An order line without an HS codeShown as "not goods-screened", never as a pass, with a link to the products that miss a code.
The check cannot run (timeout, quota)The order goes through with "not screened" and the reason in the chatter, unless you switch on "refuse when the check cannot run".

A goods result is a flag to check the item's classification. It is never a licence determination: an HS code does not decide a dual-use control, the item's technical parameters do.

Also in the app

  • Contacts: a Screen button and a status badge on every company and person, re-screened when the name, type or country changes.
  • Outgoing payments: a listed vendor is refused at posting, a potential match waits for a reviewer.
  • Vendor bank accounts: the BIC is looked up when it is saved, with the bank's name and country, and a warning when the BIC is unknown or its country differs from the IBAN's.

What it needs, and what it sends

  • An Ohmyfin account and API key. The checks are answered by Ohmyfin's API, an external service. The module is free to install and its code runs without a key; with no key and no consent it sends nothing.
  • Your consent first. Nothing leaves your database until an administrator saves the key and ticks the consent box in Settings, which names what is sent: contact names and countries, the delivery country, and each product's HS code and country of origin. No prices, quantities or documents are sent.
  • Limits. 10 checks a day with a free Ohmyfin account, 100 a day on any paid Ohmyfin plan. Screening all contacts at once and daily monitoring come with the Corporate plan.
  • Where it runs. Odoo 18.0, 19.0 and 20.0, Community and Enterprise, on-premise and Odoo.sh. Not Odoo Online, which does not install third-party apps. The goods check reads the product's "HS Code" and "Origin of Goods" fields, which Odoo adds with its shipping features (the Delivery app); without them the module checks the party and the destination, and each line reads "not goods-screened".
